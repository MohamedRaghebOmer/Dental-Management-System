# Dental Clinic Management System

A custom Arabic application that helps a dental clinic manage patients, appointments, visits, treatments, radiographs, materials, laboratory transactions, and clinic information in one organized place.

## Overview

This project was built for a real dental clinic workflow. The goal was to replace scattered notes and manual follow-up with a practical application that keeps daily operations easy to review and easier to manage.

The interface is designed for everyday clinic use, with a right-to-left Arabic layout and dedicated sections for the main areas of work.

## The Client's Need

The clinic needed a clearer way to organize its day-to-day work, reduce repetitive manual tracking, and keep important information centralized.

That included:

- patient records
- appointment scheduling and status tracking
- visit details and treatment history
- payments and outstanding balances
- radiograph records
- materials and stock monitoring
- lab transactions
- clinic profile and branding details

## Custom Freelance Solution

This was delivered as a custom freelance project for a real client.

The process started with understanding how the clinic actually works: what the staff records, what they review regularly, and what they need to see quickly during the day. Those business requirements were then translated into a tailored software solution with clear screens and workflows that match the clinic's routine.

## Key Features

### Patient Records

Keep patient information in one place, including name, age, gender, phone number, next appointment, last visit, and total visit count.

### Appointment Management

Create, update, cancel, and track appointments with clear status handling for pending, completed, missed, and canceled visits.

### Visit Management

Record both walk-in visits and appointment-based visits, with support for visit date, discount amount, notes, treatments, payments, and radiographs.

### Treatment Catalog

Maintain the clinic's treatment list and prices in a clean way, making it easier to update service information when needed.

### Laboratory Transaction Tracking

Track lab name, transaction date, total amount, paid amount, remaining amount, and treatment notes to keep outside lab work organized.

### Materials and Stock Monitoring

Manage clinic materials with visibility into quantity, reorder level, price, and stock status such as available, low stock, or out of stock.

### Radiograph Management

Store radiographs linked to visits so the clinic can review imaging records alongside the related patient and visit information.

### Clinic Branding and Profile Settings

Update the clinic name, doctor name, phone number, description, and images so the application reflects the clinic's own identity.

### Fast Search and Summary Views

Use search and filtering tools to find records quickly, while summary cards show totals and status counts at a glance.

### Controlled Activation

The application includes a device-based activation flow so the delivered software can be secured for the intended installation.

## How the Solution Works

1. The application is opened after activation.
2. The clinic can review or update its own profile information and branding.
3. Staff add new patients or search for existing ones.
4. Appointments are created and their status is tracked over time.
5. A visit is recorded either as a walk-in or from an existing appointment.
6. During the visit, staff can record treatments, payments, notes, discounts, and radiographs.
7. Materials and laboratory transactions are tracked separately so the clinic keeps a clear view of stock and external work.
8. Search tools and summary cards help staff review information quickly throughout the day.

## Designed for Growth

The solution was built with an organized structure so it can be maintained and expanded as the clinic's needs grow.

Each business area has its own workflow, which makes the application easier to support, refine, and extend without disrupting the rest of the system.
